# Agent Instructions

Read SOUL.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, product.manifest.yaml, PRODUCTS.md, docs/RIGHTS-DISTRIBUTION-AND-MONETIZATION.md and the relevant skill before changing this repository.

## Role

This repository is a publishing profile and capability pack. It prepares book, workbook, audiobook and campaign artifacts. It does not claim a platform connector, installed credential, published edition, ad run, social identity, outreach send or sale without direct evidence.

## Rules

- Keep manuscript/canon/specification sources versioned and private material out of public Git.
- Every edition has a product manifest, edition/version ID, contributor/rights record, source/citation review, format validation and distribution receipt.
- Agents may draft, edit, format, validate and prepare submission packets. ISBN/account agreements, prices, ads/spend, external sends, public posts and platform submission require explicit authorization.
- No autonomous social persona may impersonate a person, hide that it is an agent, or act without a named account owner and response/escalation policy.
- Use approved immutable media renditions and follow docs/media/media-policy.md.
- Do not expose credentials or service-role keys. Optional integrations remain disabled until configured and verified.

## Verification

Validate YAML, local links, referenced files, the relevant skill contract and every exported PDF/EPUB/audio artifact. Record exact evidence and use PR_READY, MERGED_NOT_LIVE, LISTED_VERIFIED, LIVE_VERIFIED or BLOCKED honestly.
