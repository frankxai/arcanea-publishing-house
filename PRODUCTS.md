# Publishing Product Family

| Product | Format | Customer result | Revenue path |
|---|---|---|---|
| AuthorOS Starter | workflow templates, checklists and edition ledger | organize a manuscript into a controlled production project | paid toolkit |
| Living Guide Forge | ebook, workbook and source pack | publish a useful, maintainable guide from verified knowledge | direct bundle and updates |
| World Bible Edition | lore bible, art brief, map and soundtrack packet | turn original canon into a coherent creator-ready world asset | bundle and creator license |
| Learner & Teacher Edition | learner guide, teacher guide and workbook | teach one capability with inspectable outcomes | educator and institution licenses |
| Audiobook and Trailer Packet | narration script, casting/consent, score brief, captions and trailer | prepare accessible audio and launch media | add-on and license |
| Publishing House Team Kit | roles, handoffs, rights, QA and release operations | run a small repeatable publishing studio | team license and updates |

Each product can have Starlight, FrankX or Arcanea editions only when the brand promise, audience, rights and design pack are explicit. Reusing the producer does not erase the brand boundary.
