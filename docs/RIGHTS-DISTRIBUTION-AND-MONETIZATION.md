# Rights, Distribution and Monetization

## Edition contract

Every edition records title, subtitle, edition/version, imprint/brand, author and contributors, source/citation status, manuscript checksum, cover/interior rights, illustration/model/tool provenance, narration and voice consent, ISBN or platform identifiers, territories, formats, price state, license, accessibility, platform submissions and takedown owner.

## Distribution order

1. Produce and inspect PDF/EPUB/print/audio release candidates.
2. Prove an owned offer, entitlement and durable delivery path for direct bundles.
3. Submit reader-native editions to KDP and Apple Books through their authorized accounts.
4. Use Gumroad or another reviewed Merchant of Record for cross-format toolkits and updates when margin/support make sense.
5. Generate social, trailer, email and partner packets from the exact edition metadata; external sends remain approved actions.

## Monetization

Use a value ladder: useful sample or chapter; paid book/workbook bundle; maintained toolkit or seasonal update; educator/team license; premium editorial or world adaptation at a protected scope. Books can be enriched by original music, art and cinematic web, but each component retains its own rights and accessible fallback.

Never market a draft as published, imply bestseller status, invent reviews, run ads, contact influencers or create platform/social identities without verified account authority and explicit approval.
