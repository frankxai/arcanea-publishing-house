## Proposed revisions for owner review

| Location | Finding | Proposed direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Letter V, "Your loneliness is proof of depth" | Romanticizes isolation and claims a causal relationship without support. | Replace that paragraph with: "Your loneliness does not have to prove anything about you. It may be telling you that you need company, rest, or a different kind of conversation. Take that need seriously. Depth and connection can belong to the same life." |
| Letter III, "Those who abandon you ... were never with you" | An absolute claim about other people's motives. | Replace with: "Some people may step back. Their reasons will not always be yours to know. Notice who can speak honestly with you about what happened and help you decide what to do next." |
| Letter IV, "Sometimes ... not been working hard enough" | Assumes effort explains outcomes despite differing resources and circumstances. | Revise toward a specific, observable practice gap; remove the inference about effort from another person's success. |
| Preface | Found-archive device could be mistaken for factual provenance. | Preserve the fictional device inside the text and keep the external fiction notice explicit. |
| Guardian Seraphina / Guardian Elara | Names and titles must be reconciled with the current locked canon. | Verify against `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md`; record a continuity decision without changing the lock. |

These are revision proposals. The packaged source has not silently incorporated
them. A revised release needs a new version, source hash, artifact hashes,
manifest digest and approvals.
